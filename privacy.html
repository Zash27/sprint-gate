Privacy Policy for Sprint Gate

Last Updated: August 29, 2026

Sprint Gate ("the App") is a timing tool designed for athletic training. We are committed to protecting your privacy.

Camera Usage

The App requires access to your device's camera strictly for local motion detection to trigger the timing gate. The camera feed is processed entirely on your device in real-time. We do not record, save, transmit, or share any video, images, or facial data.

Data Collection

We do not collect, store, or share any personal information, location data, or usage metrics. All timing data remains locally on your device.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer via the Google Play Store listing.
